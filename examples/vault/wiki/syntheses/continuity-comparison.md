# Comparing explanations of continuity

The [routine explanation](../concepts/routine-continuity.md) emphasizes repeatable practices. The [resource explanation](../concepts/resource-continuity.md) emphasizes reliable inputs.[^report]

The source presents competing interpretations without a test. A synthesis can map the distinction, but cannot identify a winning explanation or claim that both have been confirmed.
