# Hobby and practical reference

Use entity pages for species, equipment, places, or materials; concepts for principles; procedures for repeatable tasks; and syntheses for comparisons.

Record the applicability of guidance. A recommendation for one species or setup should not silently become a universal range or rule.

A procedure needs prerequisites, inputs, steps, expected results, failure signs, and source attribution. Explain when additional expertise is needed because the procedure's conditions are not met.

Keep dated personal experience distinct from general reference. If observations are logged over time, add the observations profile.

Do not build a public article for every equipment specification or lookup table. Clear compact reference can serve both a person and an agent.
