# Quantitative research

Keep observations separate from assumptions, models, and prose. Use the structured record contract in FORMAT.md. Include period, unit, scope, universe, method, source, locator, missingness, and relevant qualifiers.

Distinguish reported values, direct personal measurements, derived arithmetic, modeled estimates, and scenarios. Missing is not zero. Preserve inequalities and source suppression rather than reconstructing unavailable cells.

Before comparing or aggregating, check definitions, geography, dates, population universes, units, overlapping groups, and source revisions. A newly edited page is not a newly measured observation.

For demographic topics, distinguish stocks and flows, citizenship and birthplace, parental background and identification, period fertility and completed fertility, annual births and net growth. Do not substitute a convenient proxy without a documented bridge.

A model page records its question, input record paths, formula, assumptions, applicability, limitations, and revision. Use a dedicated deterministic calculation when one is needed. The starter does not evaluate model prose.

Reuse canonical observations. Do not maintain independent editable copies of a reusable figure in several pages. A sensitivity envelope is not automatically a statistical confidence interval.
